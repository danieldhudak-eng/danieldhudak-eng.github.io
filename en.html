How it works

One-off installation, simple operation by one person

See how the system works: from the fixed installation, through the cycle itself, to active monitoring.

One-off installation.

MEDIfogg® is a permanently installed automated system. Every installation is a high-tech project by our specialist team, carried out in close cooperation with the Technical Testing Institute and with minimal disruption to the facility's operation.

Show the 3 installation steps
  1. 1

    Site survey and proposal

    We walk through the rooms you want to treat and determine the number of sections and the layout of the nozzles. The result is a specific proposal for your layout, not a catalogue sheet.

  2. 2

    Fixed installation

    The piping and nozzles are installed permanently in the room. We schedule the work for a shutdown or outside the facility's operating hours.

  3. 3

    Handover and training

    We test the system, provide a test report and train the staff. Operation is at the press of a single button, so training takes one afternoon.

Operated by one person.

Operating the system requires neither an additional staff member nor any change in work organisation. Trained staff start the cycle once routine cleaning is complete.

Why it matters

Hospital-acquired infections are not a side issue

According to the ECDC, on any given day 7.1% of hospitalised patients in the EU/EEA have a hospital-acquired infection, and 6.8% in Slovakia. MEDIfogg® gives hospitals another proven tool for reducing the microbial load in the environment, which is one of the risk factors.
