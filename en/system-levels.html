System levels

Four levels from manual operation to full integration

All four levels deliver the same whole-room dry fog disinfection performance. They differ in how they are operated and in the extent of automation, sensor monitoring, data archiving, remote servicing and integration into building management systems.

Help me choose a levelFeature comparison ↓

What they have in common

A lower level does not mean lower disinfection performance

Dry fog disinfection, application of a product based on stabilised hydrogen peroxide 7.7% with ionised silver (Ag⁺), fixed installation from a single section and a five-minute application cycle are the basis of all four levels. Higher levels extend automation, monitoring, data archiving, diagnostics and integration, not disinfection efficacy.

Help me choose the right systemReporting and data records

The choice of level depends on how it is operated and the scope of record-keeping

Core is designed for simple manual operation. Control brings automatic programmable control and basic monitoring. Professional adds archiving of every cycle, extended sensors, predictive maintenance and remote servicing.

Enterprise provides comprehensive management, advanced diagnostics, cascaded coverage and BMS integration. We will propose the right level based on the extent of your premises and the requirements of your operation.
